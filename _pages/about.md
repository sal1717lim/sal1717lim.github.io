👋 I'm a PhD student in artificial intelligence at [Sorbonne Université](https://www.sorbonne-universite.fr/), working at [ISIR](https://www.isir.upmc.fr/) (Institut des Systèmes Intelligents et de Robotique) in the MLIA team, under the supervision of Prof. Laure Soulier, Prof. Olivier Sigaud and Prof. Nicolas Thome.

🤖 My research is on **multimodal agents for robotics and embodied AI**: how vision-language models and large language models can perceive, reason and act in the physical world. I work on generative models and sequential decision making.

📄 Recent work covers aligning LLM agents with reinforcement learning ([NAACL 2025 Findings](/publications/)), coupling perception and reasoning for embodied decision making ([PRISM, ICML 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05407)), and restoring the language abilities of vision-language-action models through parameter merging ([EACL 2027](/publications/)).

🏫 Before my PhD, I graduated from the Master IMA (Image) at Sorbonne Université and did a research internship in computer vision with Prof. Isabelle Bloch at LIP6.
